Skills labs

In a competition you have a few days, a problem you have never seen, and no teacher. When your model needs a method your team has never used, the clock runs while you learn it. Each of these four labs teaches one method that winning papers use, on a small problem with answers to check against, in about 45 minutes. Each ends with a contest card: the method on one screen, and what a judge expects to see.

1The four labs

  • 1Weights you can defend · the analytic hierarchy process · after Session 3
  • 2Curves from data · fitting curves with logarithms · after Session 5
  • 3Does the best team win? · simulation · after Session 6
  • 4The best of many plans · optimisation · after Session 6

Each lab opens after the session whose Maths Tool it builds on. Your coach will tell you when your team does each one.

2Which lab does my model need?

Your model needs to …MethodContest card
combine several criteria, when no data says how much each mattersComparisons two at a time (AHP)Lab 1
continue a trend that curves, or find a rate or an exponent from many pointsStraighten with logarithms, then least squaresLab 2
answer a question about chance, or give a range instead of one numberSimulation in a sheetLab 3
find the best plan, schedule or allocation, not just a better oneTry every plan; swap when there are too manyLab 4

Some problems need none of these. The simplest model that answers the question is the one to write.

3How a lab works

  • As a team, with one laptop and a Google Sheet. The Data lead types. The Modeller writes the model down before any formula is filled. The Team lead keeps the time and checks one row of the sheet by hand. The Writer writes the sentences your Team Paper would need.
  • About 45 minutes. Every lab has a problem, tasks, and "My figures" boxes to open only when your sheet has its own answer. Tasks marked "Every team" matter most; "Extra" tasks are for a team with time left.
  • Then use it. Each lab has a section on where the method fits in your Hall Problem and in a competition.
  • The Senior Team has about 20 minutes more at the end of each lab, with an optional Python version to run in Google Colab.

The numbers in the labs' small problems are made up, except the world records and the electric-vehicle figures, whose sources are given. Every figure in the "My figures" boxes was worked out again by computer.
