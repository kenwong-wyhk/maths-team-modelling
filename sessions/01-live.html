2 | The fastest human

Today's Maths Tool

Proportion as a model

time = distance ÷ speed

If the speed stays the same, twice the distance takes twice the time. That "if" is an assumption, and you chose it.

Percentage error

(model − real) ÷ real × 100%

A model says 42 s. The real time is 40 s. The error is +5%: the model is too slow.

Why a percentage and not seconds? Being 1 second out matters far more over 100 m than over 1500 m.
