2 | What you found

Three ways to continue the same dots. P is the share in per cent, t years after September 2026.

Straight line, like simple interest: 38%.

P = 20.1 + 4.25t

Compound growth, like compound interest: 81%, and over 100% during 2031.

P = 20.1 × 1.39t

Today's Maths Tool: growth with a ceiling. Let the ratio of electric to non-electric vehicles have the growth factor: 56%.

P ÷ (100 − P) = 0.252 × 1.46t

3 | AI joins the team

It is fluent, fast and sure of itself. From today you may use AI, as a teammate with named jobs.

AI may

Attack your assumptions. Help you find data, which you then trace to its source. Help with a formula or code. Check your English. Typeset your finished paper.

AI never

Chooses your model. Writes your paper. Those are yours, in your own words.

Every use goes in your team's AI Log: what you asked, what it gave, and what you did with it. In a competition the log is printed in your paper.
