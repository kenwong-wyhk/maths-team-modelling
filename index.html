Mathematical Modelling

Gentlemen, this is where we use mathematics to answer questions from the real world, where nobody holds the answer key.

1How this differs from Mathematics lessons

  • There are no marks. You get feedback on your thinking.
  • There is often more than one good answer. What matters is whether you can defend yours.
  • You work as a team. Explaining your idea to a teammate is part of the work.
  • Being wrong is useful. A model that misses tells you what to fix.

2Your Team Paper

Every team writes one paper across the term, on the Hall Problem: what is the best way to dismiss the school from the hall? From Session 3 your work is read with the Rubric, which replaces marks, and from Session 4 it must meet the Paper Standards.
